# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 0.1 (draft)

When using Orbita you must not:

- break the law or the rules of any connected platform, including advertising, disclosure, consumer-protection and intellectual-property rules;
- publish or generate content that is deceptive, defamatory, hateful, harassing, sexually exploitative, or that infringes others' rights;
- make false or unsubstantiated claims, or guarantee outcomes in regulated fields (health, finance, legal);
- connect accounts you do not own or have no written authority to manage;
- attempt to access another client's data, bypass access controls, probe or disrupt the service, or use bots or scripts against it without permission;
- scrape or collect data in ways that violate a platform's terms, collect private or non-public personal data, or use competitor research to harass or impersonate;
- upload malware or abuse file uploads;
- use the AI agent to produce spam, impersonation, or content designed to mislead people about its origin where disclosure is required;
- resell or sublicense the service without our written permission.
