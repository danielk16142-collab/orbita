# Data deletion

**Version:** 0.1 (draft)

You can remove your data from Orbita in these ways.

## Disconnect a social or ad account
In Orbita: **Client → Connections → Disconnect**. This revokes our access and **deletes the stored authorization tokens immediately**. You can also remove Orbita's access in the platform's own settings (for example Facebook/Instagram "Business Integrations", TikTok "Manage apps and services"). Metrics already collected remain in the workspace until deleted as below.

## Delete your account or workspace data
- **Users:** **Settings → Privacy → Delete my account**, or email [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]] from your account email address.
- **Clients' workspaces:** an agency administrator can offboard a client: export first, then delete. Deletion removes the brand profile, posts, metrics, conversations and files within **30 days** (backups roll off within 35 days).
- **Platform-originated data:** to delete data we received from Meta, TikTok or another platform about you, disconnect the account and email [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]] with the subject "Platform data deletion" and the account handle. We confirm completion by email.

We may keep limited records that the law requires (for example billing records, consent and rights-request records).
