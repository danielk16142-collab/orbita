# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]] · **Version:** 0.1 (draft)

This policy explains how [[COMPANY_NAME]] ("**we**", "**Orbita**") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use Orbita, a marketing management platform used by agencies and their clients. Our address: [[COMPANY_ADDRESS]]. Privacy contact: [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]] ([[PRIVACY_OFFICER]], person in charge of the protection of personal information).

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

- For **account data** (your name, email, login and usage data), we are the **controller / person responsible** for the information.
- For **client business data** (social media metrics, posts, brand information, competitor links that an agency or a business loads into Orbita), the agency or business that uses Orbita decides why and how the data is used. We process it on their behalf as a **processor / service provider**, under our [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/data-processing-agreement).

## 2. What we collect

| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Name, email, role, language, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), MFA data | You, or the agency that invited you |
| Business and brand | Business name, logo, colors, brand voice, audiences, approved claims, standing content rules | You, or your agency |
| Connected social and ad accounts | Account ID and handle, authorization tokens (stored encrypted), follower, reach, engagement and ad performance metrics, published posts and their statistics | The social or ad platform you connect, through its official API |
| Content | Posts, captions, scripts, comments, approvals, files you upload | You, your agency, and the AI agent |
| Agent conversations | Messages you exchange with the Orbita agent | You |
| Competitor information | Public links and **publicly available** information about competitor accounts | You, or public sources |
| Technical and security | IP address, device and browser data, timestamps, audit log of important actions, security-verification results (Cloudflare Turnstile) | Automatically |

We do **not** knowingly collect special categories of data (such as health or biometric data) and we do not collect private messages from your social accounts. Orbita is not intended for people under 16.

## 3. Why we use it (purposes and legal bases)

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR) / Quebec Law 25 |
|---|---|
| Provide the service: accounts, dashboards, planning, publishing, agent | Contract / necessary to provide the service |
| Connect and sync your social and ad accounts | Contract and your authorization (you can disconnect at any time) |
| Security, fraud and abuse prevention, audit logging | Legitimate interests / security obligations |
| Product support and communication about your account | Contract / legitimate interests |
| Improve the service using aggregated or de-identified usage data | Legitimate interests |

We do **not** sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 4. AI processing

Orbita's agent uses large language models provided by our service providers (see [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors)). When you use the agent, the relevant brand profile, content and messages are sent to the provider to generate a response. We do not use your data to train our own models. [[VERIFY: confirm current provider terms regarding API data retention and model training before publishing.]] The agent may produce inaccurate content. You are responsible for reviewing content before it is published. We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you using solely automated processing.

## 5. Who we share it with

- **The agency and business users** within the same client workspace, according to their role. A client user sees only their own client's data.
- **Service providers (subprocessors)** who host, secure and operate Orbita, listed on the [Subprocessors page](/legal/subprocessors), under written agreements.
- **Social and advertising platforms** (for example Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Google/YouTube) when you connect an account or publish content through Orbita.
- **Authorities or advisors** when required by law or to protect rights, and in a business transfer, with notice to you where required.

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. ## 7. How long we keep it

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Defaults are in our retention schedule:

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account data | While the account is active, then deleted or de-identified within 30 days of closure (backups roll off within 35 days) |
| Client business data, content, metrics | While the client relationship is active; deleted within 30 days after offboarding unless the agency exports it first or law requires retention |
| Social platform tokens | Deleted immediately when you disconnect an account or when the platform revokes access |
| Agent conversations | 12 months, then deleted, or earlier on request |
| Audit and security logs | 12 months |
| Records of consent and rights requests | 3 years after the event, to demonstrate compliance |

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR, Quebec Law 25, PIPEDA and California law) you may have the right to: access your data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to processing; receive a portable copy; withdraw consent; and ask how automated tools were used. Under Law 25 you may also ask that we stop disseminating or de-index information. To exercise a right, use **Settings → Privacy** in Orbita or email [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]]. We answer within **30 days** and may need to verify your identity. If your data belongs to an agency's client workspace, we may refer your request to that agency. You may complain to your data protection authority, for example the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or your EU supervisory authority.

## 9. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, encrypted storage of platform tokens, row-level access controls that separate each client's data, multi-factor authentication for agency staff, bot protection, rate limiting, audit logs and access reviews. No system is perfectly secure. If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious harm, we will notify affected people and the relevant authorities as required by law.

## 10. Cookies

We use only essential cookies (see the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie-policy)).

## 11. Social platforms and data deletion

Orbita accesses platform data only through official APIs and only for accounts you authorize. You can disconnect an account at any time in Orbita or in the platform's own settings, which revokes our access and deletes the stored tokens. To request deletion of data we obtained from a platform, follow the steps on the [Data deletion page](/legal/data-deletion).

## 12. Changes

We will update this policy when our practices change and post the new version with its date. For material changes we will notify account holders.
