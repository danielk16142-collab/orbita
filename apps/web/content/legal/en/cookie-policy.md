# Cookie Policy

**Effective date:** [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]] · **Version:** 0.1 (draft)

Orbita uses only **strictly necessary** cookies and similar storage. We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, so we do not show a consent banner. If that changes, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

| Name / purpose | Provider | Duration | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Session cookies (`sb-…`) | Supabase (authentication) | Session, refreshed while active | Keep you signed in and secure |
| Language preference | Orbita | Up to 1 year | Remember your language (English, French, Spanish) |
| Security verification | Cloudflare Turnstile | Short-lived | Tell people and bots apart on sign-in and forms |

Turnstile is provided by Cloudflare, Inc. and runs a challenge in your browser; see Cloudflare's privacy information for how it handles that data. You can block cookies in your browser, but sign-in will not work without the session cookies.
